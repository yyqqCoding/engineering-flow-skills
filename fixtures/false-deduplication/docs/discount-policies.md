# Discount Policies

Shipping discounts are a logistics campaign owned by the fulfillment team. Orders below 100 do not qualify. Campaign rates and caps may change at any time.

Loyalty discounts are a customer-benefit rule owned by the membership team. Orders below 100 do not qualify. Its current rate is 5% and its cap is 25.

The two policies happen to use the same arithmetic today but change independently and must remain understandable without coordinating releases.
